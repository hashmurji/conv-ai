Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

Conv-AI is a trading name of Lucere TG Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 12995725. Our registered office is Carlyle House, 78 Chorley New Road, Bolton, Greater Manchester, BL1 4BY.

Lucere TG Ltd is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we use your information, contact us at hello@conv-ai.co.uk.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we use personal data when you visit this website, contact us, or ask to join the Conv-AI focus group.

It does not cover documents and client information that law firms and other customers upload to the Conv-AI platform. In that case the customer is the controller and we act on their instructions as a processor, under a separate written agreement.

3. The information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you use our contact form we collect your name, email address, firm or organisation, the type of enquirer you are, your approximate monthly volume of title reviews, whether you would like to join the focus group, and anything you write in your message.
  • Correspondence. If you email or speak to us, we keep a record of that correspondence.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider automatically records limited technical data when you visit the site, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use this to keep the site secure and working.

We do not ask for special category data through this website. Please do not include it, or any confidential client information, in your message.

4. How we use your information and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your enquiry and send you a confirmation email. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To run the focus group and pilot, including contacting you to arrange calls and early access. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in developing the platform with the people who asked to take part.
  • To keep you updated about Conv-AI, where you have asked us to or where the law allows. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and working. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in operating a safe, reliable site.
  • To meet our legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website and our business, acting on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website and provides its domain name services.
  • Resend, which delivers contact form submissions to us and the emails we send from the website.
  • Google, which provides the Gmail inbox where we read and reply to email.
  • Google Analytics, if you accept analytics cookies, which tells us how the site is used.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typeface used on this site. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which involves sharing your IP address with Google.
  • Our professional advisers, and regulators or authorities where we are legally required to.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers are based outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where your personal data is transferred outside the UK, we make sure it is protected by an appropriate safeguard, such as UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and focus group information for 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer or ask us to delete it sooner. Technical logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider.

8. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. Where we use analytics, we use Google Analytics, and only after you accept analytics cookies. It sets cookies that tell us which pages are visited and how people find the site, and it does not tell us who you are.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of the page. We also store your cookie choice in your browser so that we do not ask you again.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your personal data;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it;
  • ask us to transfer your data to you or another organisation;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email hello@conv-ai.co.uk. We will respond within one month. There is normally no charge.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
