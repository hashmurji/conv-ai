    We built the tool we wanted on our own files.

    Conv-AI started with a familiar problem: a stack of official copies, a client waiting for an explanation, and not enough hours in the day. Official copies, filed deeds, old conveyances and leases all have to be read, checked and explained in a way the client understands.

    We are practising conveyancers who have spent years doing exactly that, working with a product team that builds data platforms for regulated entities governed by various regulators including the FCA, SRA and CLC. The platform is built, and we are piloting shortly before opening to law firms.
